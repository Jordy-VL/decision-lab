# CE + AURC continuation: corrected evaluation

Run `aurc-20260923-115913` completed one weights-only continuation from the frozen CE checkpoint, with a fresh AdamW optimizer and CE + 0.5 detached harmonic rank-weighted CE. It trained for one epoch (3,894 updates) on the same 15,576 Kev decision-v7 training rows. The run and corrected evaluations used development and calibration only; test data was not mounted or read.

## Evaluation correction

The first evaluation invocation accidentally kept the `--checkpoint` argument pointing to the CE parent. Its reports exactly reproduced the parent metrics and are superseded. An eval-only Modal pass loaded the new AURC checkpoint and explicitly selected the development and calibration partitions. Corrected reports and predictions are retained under `runs/aurc-20260923-115913/{development,calibration}-corrected/` in the ignored local run directory and on the Modal Volume. The launcher now evaluates the trained checkpoint in future runs.

Metrics are pooled per-question values. AURC is discrete mean error risk over coverages 1/N through 1, with exact confidence ties averaged. “Calibration partition” identifies the dataset partition; probabilities have not been temperature-scaled or otherwise calibrated.

Against the CE warm-up, the continuation has higher accuracy and lower AURC on both partitions, but also higher NLL. This is a preliminary parent-to-continuation comparison, **not an isolated estimate of the AURC loss effect**: the AURC model received another epoch of training, and there is not yet a matched CE continuation with the same data, seed, fresh optimizer and update count. Do not claim a causal AURC-training benefit until that arm is run. Results also remain single-seed and pooled; inspect per-type/source metrics and uncertainty before interpreting small differences.
